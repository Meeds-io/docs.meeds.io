# ✅ Tracking your contributions

### :question: How to find your previous contributions ?

#### :point\_right: From the Dashboard

* Check the details of your achievements from your dashboard. You will find the list of your previous contributions.&#x20;
* You can also filter them by campaign.

#### :point\_right: From the 'Contributions' Page

* Access a summary table of your contributions from the dedicated 'Dashboard' page.&#x20;
* You can filter by period, campaign, or action.&#x20;
* You have the flexibility to sort by date, status, or type of contribution,&#x20;
* And you can export the data in .XLSX format.

#### 💡 Note

* Any quest canceled by you (manually or automatically) will be considered canceled in the list of contributions.
* An quest rejected by the campaign owner will also be reflected as such in your tracking table.
* Quests that are pending are not considered in the leaderboard nor in the rewarding
* Accepted contributions will be considered for the rewarding period during it has been accepted
