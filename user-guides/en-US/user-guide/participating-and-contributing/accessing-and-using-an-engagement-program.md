# 👀 Viewing campaigns

{% hint style="info" %}
This page applies to the **Business** and **Enterprise** plans.  [👉 Compare all plans](https://www.meeds.io/pricing)
{% endhint %}

### :question:What are we talking about?

* Your community leader can regroup quests into campaigns,&#x20;
* And these campaigns can also target a specific audience.
* Some campaigns may be restricted to a space, meaning only members of that space can contribute to the proposed quest

### :question:Where to find the campaigns?

* The dashboard lists the most recently updated campaigns.&#x20;
* You can also access the complete list of campaigns on the dedicated page.

### &#x20;:question:What does a campaign present?

#### :point\_right: For each campaign, find its specific characteristics:

* A general description explaining the expected objectives.
* The list of campaign owners.
* The audience for this campaign.

#### :point\_right: List of available quests and more.

* By default, only currently available quests are listed.&#x20;
* If needed, filter quests based on their status: completed, coming soon.

### :question: Who hosts the campaigns?

* If your campaign is associated with a space, the space hosts are automatically responsible.
* If your campaign is open to all members, reward administrators are responsible.

:bulb: Additional campaign **owners** can be appointed if needed to delegate the hosting of contributions.
