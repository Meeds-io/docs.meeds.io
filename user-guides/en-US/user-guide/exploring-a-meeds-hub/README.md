# 🚀 Exploring a Hub

{% hint style="info" %}
This page applies to the **Business** and **Enterprise** plans. [👉 Compare all plans](https://www.meeds.io/pricing)
{% endhint %}

Follow below our practical tips to get started successfully

### :question:Need to navigate to other pages

**👉 Open the left menu and navigate the platform with ease to:**

* View and edit your [Profile](../setting-up-your-account/updating-your-profile.md)
* Explore actions available on the [Contribute site](contribution-center.md)
* Enter your [Workspace ](my-workspace.md)(activity feed, tasks, content)
* Browse the [directory of members and spaces](people-and-spaces.md)
* Modify your [User Settings](../setting-up-your-account/notifications-preferences.md)
* Read Terms & Conditions from user settings (if published by admins)&#x20;

### 💡 Note

* Pin the left menu as per your preference and keep it open
* Personalize your homepage from the left menu
